# Mechapede

[Play Mechapede](https://davbachman.github.io/Mechapede/)

Created by David Bachman with GPT-6 Astra. To learn more about David see [his website](https://pzacad.pitzer.edu/~dbachman/), and subscribe to his AI blog *[Entropy Bonus](https://profbachman.substack.com/)*.

## Brief description

A mechanical arcade game inspired by Centipede. Pilot a cutting tool inside a broken machine, blast through gear-driven conveyor units, and dodge runaway maintenance equipment.

## Instructions for use

- Press **Enter** or click **Engage Drive** to start and capture the pointer.
- Move with your trackpad or mouse. Fast flicks build momentum; gentle or opposite movements brake. Adjust sensitivity beside the board.
- Hold **Space** or the primary mouse button to fire. **WASD** and arrow keys also move the tool.
- Press **P** or **Escape** to pause and release the pointer. Press **P** or **Enter** to resume.
- Press **M** to mute or **F** for fullscreen. Scores, sensitivity and audio settings are saved in your browser.
- If pointer capture is unavailable, choose **Use Window / Keyboard Controls**. Chrome is recommended for captured trackball control on a Mac.
